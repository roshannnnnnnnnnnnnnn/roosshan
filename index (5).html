What the figures suggest

In these published 2024 estimates, MEGA MGC COFFEE has the highest average annual sales figure, followed by Paik’s Coffee and COMPOSE COFFEE. This is a comparison of reported averages only; store size, location, operating period, delivery mix, and calculation methods can affect the figures.

Search history: four queries

The assignment asks students to search each query separately and compare what appears. The notes below describe typical differences in result types; actual Google results can vary by date, location, and personalization.

1. General web search국내 프랜차이즈 매출 순위

Usually returns news articles, blogs, and ranking summaries. Check whether the figure refers to company revenue, franchisee sales, or sales per store.

일반 검색은 뉴스·블로그·순위 요약이 많이 나옵니다. 본사 매출인지, 가맹점 매출인지, 점포당 매출인지 구분해야 합니다.
2. PDF documents국내 프랜차이즈 매출 순위 filetype:pdf

Can surface reports and official disclosure documents, which may include tables and base-year details. PDFs may be older than their upload or search result date suggests.

PDF 검색은 보고서나 정보공개서 표를 찾는 데 유용합니다. 게시·업로드 날짜와 실제 매출 기준연도는 다를 수 있습니다.
3. Naver-hosted pages국내 프랜차이즈 매출 순위 site:naver.com

Narrows results to Naver-hosted pages, such as blogs or posts. These can provide context but may repeat figures without linking the original source.

네이버 페이지로 범위를 좁히면 블로그나 게시글이 주로 나옵니다. 원자료 링크가 있는지 확인하고 재인용된 수치는 주의해야 합니다.
4. Date-limited search국내 프랜차이즈 매출 순위 after:2024-06-01 before:2026-01-01

Limits results to a publication window. A post published in 2025 may still report 2023 or 2024 sales, so record both dates separately.

게시 기간을 제한할 수 있지만, 2025년에 게시된 글이 2023년 또는 2024년 실적을 다룰 수도 있습니다. 게시일과 데이터 기준연도를 따로 기록하세요.

Sources and verification notes

The brand-specific values above were cross-checked against public summaries that say they use franchise disclosure data. For the strongest verification, open the official Fair Trade Commission franchise disclosure portal and inspect each brand’s original disclosure for the 2024 business year.

  1. Korea Fair Trade Commission — Franchise Business Information Disclosure System (official portal; search each brand and confirm the original document/year)
  2. MEGA MGC COFFEE — disclosure summary (2024 annual sales estimate: ₩388.443 million)
  3. COMPOSE COFFEE — disclosure summary (2024 average annual sales: ₩271.883 million)
  4. Paik’s Coffee — disclosure summary (2024 estimated average annual sales: approximately ₩324.486 million)
  5. ZDNet Korea — report on franchise store counts and average sales (published April 12, 2026; useful context, not the 2024 primary disclosure)

Research integrity note: the official portal is provided as the primary verification destination. The linked brand summaries are secondary sources, so check the original documents before treating every value as fully verified. If an original figure cannot be confirmed, mark it “Unverified” rather than guessing.
